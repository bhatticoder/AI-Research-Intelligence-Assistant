# Project Orion: Deep Space Habitation Study

## Executive Summary
Project Orion is a fictional (for testing purposes) aerospace initiative started in 2024. The primary goal is to establish a permanent human presence on Jupiter's moon, Europa, by the year 2045. 

## Key Personnel
- **Dr. Aris Thorne**: Chief Astrophysicist and Lead Director of Project Orion.
- **Captain Elena Rostova**: Commander of the Vanguard-1 exploratory vessel.
- **Dr. Kenji Sato**: Head of Cryogenics and Life Support Systems.

## Technical Specifications of Vanguard-1
The Vanguard-1 spacecraft is equipped with a revolutionary "Helios Core" fusion drive, allowing it to reach Europa in just 14 months instead of the standard 5 years. It features triple-redundant radiation shielding made from a newly synthesized material called "Aegis-Plast".

## Sub-surface Ocean Exploration
Europa's icy crust is estimated to be 15 kilometers thick. Project Orion plans to deploy the "Nautilus-7" thermal drill to melt through the ice and release a swarm of autonomous hydro-bots to search for microbial life in the subsurface ocean. The primary bio-signature they are looking for is a hypothetical organism classified as "Europan Methanogen Type-B".
